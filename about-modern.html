About Our Company

Excellence in construction and engineering since our foundation

Our Story

Basaltos y Agregados S.A. de C.V. was born with the extensive experience of its staff, which has the ability to offer an optimal, efficient, and specialized service to its customers. Our technical staff is highly trained and has participated in various large-scale works, specializing in land, sea, and structures.

Today, our field of action is very competitive in the new stage that our country is experiencing in the oil and construction industry. This situation makes us more aware of providing quality services and adhering to environmental regulations.

We remain with the constant commitment to update ourselves in the different areas that involve the construction of different types of works where we participate. We constantly train our staff with new technologies and cutting-edge equipment to maintain our position as leaders in the industry.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide high-quality construction and engineering services that exceed our clients' expectations, while maintaining the highest standards of safety, environmental responsibility, and professional excellence. We are committed to building sustainable infrastructure that contributes to the development of our communities.

Our Vision

To be recognized as the leading construction and engineering company in Mexico, known for our innovation, reliability, and commitment to excellence. We aspire to set industry standards through continuous improvement, technological advancement, and unwavering dedication to quality.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every project we undertake

Quality First

We ensure that each and every one of the people who collaborate internally or externally with the company maintain efficient, reliable, and above all, transparent quality work.

Professional Integrity

We have the obligation to observe the quality policy, regulations, and internal procedures and refrain from participating in activities that discredit the company.

Honest Service

We guarantee that all the services offered by the company are carried out with honesty, integrity, seriousness, and above all, quality.

Transparent Decision Making

We guarantee that each and every one of the decisions about processes are taken with equity, impartiality, transparency, and objectivity based solely on identified needs.

Regulatory Compliance

We observe the policies, regulations, and procedures in the operation of work processes and strive to consolidate the company's image through quality services.

Ethical Standards

We refrain from participating with third parties interested in carrying out transactions that entail the collection of irregular or illegal fees.
