Our Background

Today our field of action looks very competitive in the new stage that our country is experiencing in the oil and construction industry. This situation makes us more aware of providing quality services and adhering to environmental regulations.

At Basaltos y Agregados S.A. de C.V., we remain with the constant commitment to update ourselves in the different areas that involve the construction of different types of works where we participate. We constantly train our staff with new technologies and cutting-edge equipment.

Our company was born with extensive experience of its staff, which has the ability to offer an optimal, efficient and specialized service to its customers. Our technical staff is highly trained and has participated in various large-scale works, specializing in land, sea and structures.
